# Profile and card images

Place your own image files in this folder, then point to them in
`assets/js/config.js`.

Recommended sizes:

- `profile.webp`: square or portrait, at least 800 × 800
- `card.webp`: portrait, around 1200 × 1600
- `cover.webp`: square, at least 500 × 500 (optional music cover)

PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, and full hosted/CDN URLs are supported. Put a hosted URL
in `remote`; put a repository path in `local`.
